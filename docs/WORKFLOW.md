# Learning toward a goal

## Define an observable outcome

Start with what the learner wants to do, explain, build or decide independently, in what context and by when. Record starting evidence, resources and constraints. An exam grade is an aspiration; a prototype is an artifact; neither is automatically proof of transferable knowledge. Write success criteria that can be assessed.

For a parser goal, a criterion might be: parse unseen expressions with precedence, explain a syntax error, and justify the design without step-by-step help. For an exam, use the official scope and representative independent tasks across the whole syllabus.

## Build the map before selecting depth

Inventory authoritative resources, relevant task requirements and prerequisites. For an academic course, cross-check all lecture outlines, tutorials and stated scope. For a project, derive capabilities from acceptance criteria and consult reliable references. If resources are missing, research them; do not pretend to have read inaccessible material.

Record topic source IDs and page/section anchors. Map sample tasks to topics. Samples never replace the complete map. Mark unresolved scope as unknown; exclude topics only with a reason and evidence. Review the inventory manually before marking it reviewed. Do not generate all future lesson content: plan the route, then prepare only the current part.

## Agree on real tradeoffs

Use the time budget to prioritize transparently. When the full goal cannot fit, show what will be omitted or left shallow and ask the learner to choose a reduced outcome. Asking how far along we are is not a request to accelerate. Preserve an understandable pace and make partial completion visible.

## Allocate the next activity

Follow [TOPIC-ALLOCATION.md](TOPIC-ALLOCATION.md). Resolve scope and prerequisites, inspect required coverage gaps, then balance uncertainty checks and practice of known weaknesses. Sample apparently strong topics periodically. Use the read-only `plan` helper when available and record its recommendation or your reason for overriding it. Do not infer a weakness from a topic that has not been assessed.

## Teach one connected part

Bridge from what the learner has demonstrated. Define unfamiliar terms, explain the mechanism, and work one complete example. Then ask a manageable question with a rubric frozen before delivery. Asking a beginner about unintroduced knowledge is a teaching failure, not evidence of poor learning. Prior knowledge can be diagnosed gently without treating untaught content as an exam failure.

As ability grows, fade assistance and introduce changes of context. Use actual artifacts and external feedback for project, creative or physical skills. A text tutor cannot verify physical execution or replace qualified supervision where required.

## Record evidence honestly

Keep exact learner responses separate from tutor interpretations. Store question, source basis, frozen rubric, date, hint level and observed result in private assessment notes. Distinguish guided, independent, transferable and delayed evidence. Withdraw flawed assessments and preserve the correction history. Do not infer a misconception from ambiguous wording without clarification.

Coverage values: not_started, introduced, worked_example, practiced. In this ledger, worked_example and practiced satisfy the delivered-coverage threshold. They do not establish understanding. Use only what was actually delivered, not generated files.

Evidence values: unknown, guided, independent, transferable, durable. Reference the actual record using evidence_ref. Transfer requires an unfamiliar task; durable requires observed delayed recall. A scheduler due date is not evidence of forgetting, and a correct coached response is not independent mastery.

## Audit before claiming completion

Reconcile all required capabilities and source headings against the map. Run the coverage audit and inspect exclusions and gaps. Then test the goal independently against its success criteria. Record incomplete criteria openly. This CLI does not implement a goal-completion gate; the tutor must inspect the evidence rather than infer success from an audit pass.

## Resume and improve

Save the exact position, unresolved questions and next action. Retrieve before showing old answers on later reviews. Ask for recall difficulty instead of inventing it. After use, record failures and useful behavior, turn confirmed problems into rules or checks, and test changes with the behavioral evaluation cases.

For exam-specific source reconciliation and logistics, use EXAM-WORKFLOW.md.
